01 / THE BUILDING BLOCKS

Meet the logic circuit ICs

Logic gates are the decision-makers inside digital systems. Integrated circuits (ICs) bring these gates together in compact chips. The 74xx TTL examples below use a 14-pin package: pin 14 connects to VCC (+5 V), while pin 7 connects to ground (GND).

ICGateGates per chipOutput
7404NOT (inverter)6Y = A'
7408AND4Y = A · B
7432OR4Y = A + B
7400NAND4Y = (A · B)'
7402NOR4Y = (A + B)'
7486XOR4Y = A ⊕ B
